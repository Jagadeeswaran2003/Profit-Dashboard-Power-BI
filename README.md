# Profit Dashboard Power BI
The Profit Dashboard offers a detailed view of business performance across various dimensions. Total profit stands at 286.4K with 2.30M in sales and 38K in quantity sold. The West region is the most profitable, while the Central region lags behind. Among customer segments, the Consumer segment leads in profit generation. In terms of categories, Technology contributes the highest profit, followed by Office Supplies, with Furniture bringing in the least. Sub-category analysis shows Copiers and Phones are top performers, while Tables and Bookcases result in losses. Ship Mode analysis reveals Standard Class generates the most profit and sales. Discounting is minimal, totaling only 1.56K, which may indicate efficient pricing. The visuals provide clarity on which products and strategies are driving profitability. This insight helps identify areas to expand and segments needing improvement.
